SMALL APP. YOUR MAC.

Make yourself
at home.

No account, analytics, or server. TopChrome manages its own Chrome window; it does not copy your profile or read your browsing history.

Allow TopChrome to control Google Chrome when macOS asks. For desktop following, choose Desktop Following Setup → Enable for All Chrome Windows. This moves your regular Chrome windows too, preserving their tabs. Following supports normal desktops and may need an update after future macOS releases.

Pair with TabShelf to open saved sites straight into the drawer.

Build locally with Swift 6 and the Xcode command-line tools. The development bundle is ad-hoc signed. Signed and notarized downloads will be published separately.
